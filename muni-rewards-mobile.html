FAQs

Everything you need to know about earning and redeeming points.

How do I earn points?

You earn points on every purchase. Scan your app or give your phone number at checkout. Points appear in your account instantly.

When do points expire?

Points expire after 12 months of inactivity. Make a purchase to keep your balance active. We will send a reminder before anything disappears.

How do I redeem rewards?

Open the app and tap the Rewards tab. Select your reward and show the code at the register. You can also redeem online at checkout.

How do tiers work?

You start at Bronze. Earn 500 points to reach Silver and 1500 for Gold. Higher tiers earn points faster and unlock better perks.

Can I use points for delivery?

Yes. Apply your points at checkout for pickup or delivery. Some rewards are pickup only. The app will show what is available.
