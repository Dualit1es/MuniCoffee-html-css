FAQs

Quick answers for account issues, password resets, and order lookups. We keep it simple.

Forgot your password?

Click the forgot password link on the sign in form. We will email you a reset link. The link expires in 30 minutes.

How do I check my rewards balance?

Sign in to your account and open the dashboard. Your points and tier appear at the top. It updates after every order.

Can I look up an order?

Yes. Go to your account dashboard and select order history. You can view past orders and reorder your favorites.

Why can't I sign in?

Check your email and password for typos. If it still fails, reset your password or contact support.

Is my account information secure?

We encrypt all personal data and never sell your information. Payments are processed through secure providers.

Our support team is here to help you get back to your coffee.
